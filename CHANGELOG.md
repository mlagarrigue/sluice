### ⚠ BREAKING CHANGES

* **web:** verify tokens as RFC 7519 and 8725 ask
* **parallel:** a worker's runtime error keeps its stack
* **core:** FlatMap appends into the output batch
* **diagnostics:** Demand carries no column set
* **postgres:** move the codec to pgtype
* **postgres:** ADR 0022, five internals unexported
* **quic:** varints move to an internal package
* **web:** the Bearer challenge names its realm
* **quic:** retire BBR version 1
* **httpstream:** ServeH2Conn and ServeH3Conn serve one connection
* **core:** Overflow and Completion have no zero policy
* **web:** a zero shutdown grace is five seconds
* **web:** stream stages take the batch first, limit int64
* **quic:** one Dial, Credit on OnStreamFrames, WriteFinal
* **postgres:** Param, Config.StatementCacheSize, sealed Rows
* **parallel:** rename WithState to OrderedWithState
* **join:** take an IntervalSpec, drop the storage seam
* **core:** panic on a Split route index out of range
* **postgres:** budget the remembered parameters together
* **postgres:** choose or disable SCRAM channel binding
* **quic:** tune the Listener, keep its keys

  `*Router` alone, no error: drop the `err` from `r, err := NewRouter(...)`;

  shadows, now panics at start-up, where the mistake is.
